Welcome to **CollabDocs**, your clean, focused collaborative document editor. This guide will walk you through everything you need to create, edit, import, and share documents with your team.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 Creating an Account (Registration)
1. Navigate to the CollabDocs homepage.
2. Click **Create an account** at the bottom of the sign-in card.
3. Enter your **Full Name**, **Work Email**, and choose a secure **Password** (minimum 6 characters).
4. Click **Create Account**. You will be logged in immediately to your personal workspace.

### 1.2 Signing In
1. Enter your registered email address and password.
2. Click **Sign In**.
3. If you have not created an account yet, click **Create an Account** to register your profile.

## 2. Managing Your Documents

### 2.1 Creating a New Document
1. In the left sidebar or the top dashboard header, click **Create Document** (or **+ New** on mobile).
2. Enter a descriptive title (e.g., `Product Strategy 2026`).
3. Click **Create Document**. The editor will open immediately, ready for you to type.

### 2.2 Renaming a Document
- **From the Dashboard**: Hover over any document card you own, click the three-dots menu (**⋮**), choose **Rename**, enter the new title, and click **Save Title**.
- **Inside the Editor**: Click on the document title in the top-left navigation bar, type your new title, and press **Enter** (or click away to save).
- *Note: Only the document owner can rename documents.*

### 2.3 Deleting a Document
1. On the dashboard, locate the document card you own.
2. Click the options menu (**⋮**) in the top right of the card.
3. Click **Delete**.
4. Confirm the deletion in the prompt. The document and its sharing records will be permanently removed.

## 3. Writing and Formatting

CollabDocs features a distraction-free, professional rich-text editor powered by Tiptap.

### 3.1 Formatting Toolbar
Use the top formatting toolbar to apply formatting to selected text:
- **Headings**: Click the **H1** or **H2** icons to create hierarchy. Click the paragraph symbol (**¶**) to return to body text.
- **Emphasis**:
  - **B**: Bold (`Ctrl+B` or `Cmd+B`)
  - **I**: Italic (`Ctrl+I` or `Cmd+I`)
  - **U**: Underline (`Ctrl+U` or `Cmd+U`)
- **Lists**:
  - Click the bullet list icon for unnumbered bulleted points.
  - Click the numbered list icon for ordered step-by-step instructions.
- **Alignment**: Align text left, center, or right.
- **History**: Use the Undo (`Ctrl+Z`) and Redo (`Ctrl+Y`) buttons to revert or reapply changes.

### 3.2 Autosave and Manual Save
- **Autosave**: As you type, CollabDocs automatically saves your changes after you pause for 1 second.
- **Save Status Indicators**:
  - `Saving...`: Document changes are currently being synced to the database.
  - `Saved`: All changes are safely persisted.
  - `Unsaved changes`: You have made edits that are pending autosave.
  - `Save failed`: A network interruption occurred. Click the retry link.
- **Manual Save**: You can press `Ctrl+S` (or `Cmd+S` on Mac) or click the **Save** button in the header at any time to save immediately.

## 4. Importing Documents (.md and .txt)

You can bring your existing documents and notes into CollabDocs:
1. In the dashboard sidebar, click **Import .md / .txt**.
2. Either drag and drop your file into the dashed box or click the box to browse your computer.
3. Supported formats:
   - **Markdown (`.md`, `.markdown`)**: Automatically converts headings (`#`, `##`), bold (`**`), italic (`*`), and lists into rich-text elements.
   - **Plain Text (`.txt`)**: Preserves paragraphs and formatting breaks.
4. Review the detected elements and customize the document title if desired.
5. Click **Import to Editor**. A new document will be created and opened immediately.

## 5. Sharing and Collaboration

### 5.1 Sharing a Document with a Teammate
1. Open the document you wish to share (you must be the owner).
2. In the top-right header, click **Share**.
3. Enter the registered email address of your teammate (or click one of the quick test account pills).
4. Select the permission level:
   - **Editor**: Can view, edit, and format content. Cannot delete the document or rename it.
   - **Viewer**: Read-only access. Can read the document, but cannot edit text, apply formatting, or rename the document.
5. Click **Share**. The recipient will now see the document in their **Shared With Me** dashboard tab.

### 5.2 Changing Collaborator Permissions
1. Open the **Share** dialog on an owned document.
2. In the collaborator list, use the dropdown next to any teammate to change their role between **Editor** and **Viewer**. Changes take effect immediately.

### 5.3 Revoking Access
1. In the **Share** dialog, locate the teammate whose access you wish to revoke.
2. Click the red trash icon (**🗑**) next to their name. Access will be revoked immediately.

---

## 6. Signing Out

To log out:
1. Click your user profile in the top-right navbar.
2. Click **Sign Out**. Your session will be securely cleared.
